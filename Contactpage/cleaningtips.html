Cleaning Tips

Simple habits to keep your space fresh between GPAUTA visits.

Between-Visit Habits

  • Wipe down kitchen counters and stovetops daily to prevent buildup
  • Air out rooms for a few minutes each day to reduce moisture and odors
  • Empty small trash bins before they overflow
  • Keep entryways clear of dirt with a doormat and quick daily sweep

Before Your Cleaning Appointment

Clear clutter from surfaces and floors so our team can focus on deep cleaning rather than tidying — you'll get more thorough results from every visit.
