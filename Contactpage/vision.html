Our Vision

The future we're working toward for Buea's homes, businesses, and environment.

A Cleaner, Greener Buea

We envision a Buea where no recyclable material ends up in a landfill, where every home and business has access to reliable, affordable cleaning services, and where sustainable waste habits are simply part of everyday life.

GPAUTA exists to close the gap between good intentions and convenient action — making it easy for our community to recycle responsibly and keep their spaces spotless, without extra effort or expense.

Looking Ahead

  • Expand recycling pickup coverage across all neighborhoods in Buea
  • Partner with local schools and businesses on sustainability education
  • Reduce landfill-bound waste from our service area year over year
  • Build Cameroon's most trusted name in eco-conscious cleaning
