About Us

Our Mission

What we do every day to serve Buea, one pickup and one clean space at a time.

Our Purpose

GPAUTA Recycling and Cleaning Service was founded to give households and businesses in Buea a dependable, affordable way to manage waste responsibly and keep their spaces clean — without compromising on convenience or the environment.

How We Deliver On It

Collect

Responsible Waste Collection

Reliable pickup schedules for recyclables and general waste, sorted and processed correctly from the start.

Clean

Trusted Cleaning Services

Thorough, consistent cleaning for homes and businesses, delivered by a trained and vetted team.

Educate

Community Awareness

Practical guidance on sorting, reducing waste, and adopting habits that keep Buea cleaner long-term.
