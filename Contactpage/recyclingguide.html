Recycling Guide

What to sort, how to prep it, and what we can and can't collect.

Sorting Basics

  • Paper & Cardboard — keep dry, flatten boxes
  • Plastics — rinse containers, remove caps if a different material
  • Glass — rinse, remove lids
  • Metal — empty and rinse cans before collection

What We Can't Accept

Food waste, hazardous materials, electronics, and heavily soiled items cannot go in your recycling pickup. Contact us if you're unsure about a specific item.
